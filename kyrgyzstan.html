The project

Four landscapes.
Four ways to listen.

Kyrgyzstan is a generative sound journey through elemental and urban environments. Each soundscape explores a different sense of place — from the open air of the steppe to the intimate textures of earth and water, and the quiet pulse of Bishkek.

The project combines generative synthesis with a visual listening map. Every zone can be opened directly from this page or reached through its own QR code, connecting the listener to a distinct sonic atmosphere.

Listen to the contours of a place — its distance, movement and quiet details.
