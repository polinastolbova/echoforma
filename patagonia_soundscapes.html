Patagonia — Soundscapes

What does Patagonia sound like? Not just the wind or the silence, but the feeling of vastness, the slow movement of clouds, the distant hum of mountains, and the fleeting shimmer of light.

Six placesOne landscape, interpreted through sound
The exhibition

Listening to the shape of a place.

This project explores how the essence of Patagonia can be translated into sound using Csound, a language for sound synthesis and composition.

Each soundscape is an interpretation rather than a field recording: a study of atmosphere, scale, movement and presence. The compositions are built from generated tones, noise, modulation and space.
