The project

A sound route through the museum.

The project explores how a museum apartment can be experienced through generative sound. Seven rooms form a single acoustic route, where sound, silence and anticipation shape the visitor's movement.

Built with Csound, stochastic processes and procedural generation, the works create abstract acoustic situations: a doorbell, a social field of bells, the rhythm of writing, near silence and finally a light, fluid texture in the study.
