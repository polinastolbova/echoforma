The project

Objects become points of listening.

What if a museum could be heard as well as seen? Zhiganov Museum is a generative soundscape imagined as an additional, intimate layer of the museum visit.

QR codes placed beside selected objects invite visitors to scan with a phone and listen. Each code opens a sound piece connected to a zone: the writing desk, the piano, decorative objects, or the composer’s room. Sound does not replace the object or its history; it offers another way to spend time with it.

A small gesture — scan, listen, notice the room again.
