Van-Gogh

Three generative soundscapes inspired by landscapes of Vincent van Gogh.

The project explores how colour, vegetation, rural space and movement can be translated from painting into sound — not as illustration, but as another way of experiencing the landscape.

Landscape with a house and a plowman · 1889♪
The project

Painting translated into listening.

What happens when a landscape is approached through sound instead of sight? These works begin with Van Gogh's painted environments and translate their perceived qualities into generative soundscapes.

Fields, bushes, houses, distance, vegetation and movement become timbre, density, rhythm and spatial depth.

Not a soundtrack to the paintings, but a way to experience their atmosphere through sound.
