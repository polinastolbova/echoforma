About

The Language
of Energy

Vincent van Gogh’s paintings are not just visual landscapes, but flows of energy — rhythms, tensions and movements. In EchoForma, these visual impulses are reimagined as generative soundscapes, where colour, gesture and texture become sound.

Each piece is a meeting point between art, technology and sound — an attempt to hear what is usually seen.
