EchoForma · Soundscapes

Van- Gogh

Three generative soundscapes inspired by landscapes of Vincent van Gogh.

The project explores how colour, vegetation, rural space and movement can be translated from painting into sound — not as illustration, but as another way of experiencing the landscape.

The project

Painting translated into listening.

What happens when a landscape is approached through sound instead of sight? These works begin with Van Gogh's painted environments and translate their perceived qualities into generative soundscapes.

Fields, bushes, houses, distance, vegetation and movement become timbre, density, rhythm and spatial depth.

Not a soundtrack to the paintings, but a way to experience their atmosphere through sound.

01 / 03

Landscape with a house and a plowman

Fields, a stable architectural centre, distant hills and the measured movement of a figure are translated into a layered acoustic landscape.

“movement through an open landscape”
Sound structure +
  • Fields soft harmonic foundation · 110 Hz
  • House stable saw-tooth centre · gentle wobble
  • Hills very slow low-frequency wave
  • Walker 1.2 Hz soft pulse
  • Cypresses short high-frequency strokes

02 / 03

Bush

A dense bush becomes a field of soft harmonic mass, tiny points of brightness and irregular painterly strokes. The sound remains gentle and continuously alive.

“density, colour and small movements”
Sound structure +
  • Sky soft pastel waves · 70 Hz
  • Path almost imperceptible 0.6 Hz rhythm
  • Lilac mass 220 Hz harmonic texture
  • Flowers small 1400 Hz points
  • Strokes short 500 Hz gestures
  • Melody slow seven-note sequence

03 / 03

A house with huts

The rural scene is built from a soft environmental foundation and a few more defined elements: grass, houses, smoke, flowers and short brush-like gestures.

“a quiet rural scene with a visible centre”
Sound structure +
  • Sky soft harmonic waves · 90 Hz
  • Grass granular sine texture · 300–700 Hz
  • Houses three saw waves · 160–190 Hz
  • Smoke slowly rising 200–600 Hz tone
  • Flowers 3 Hz bright points
  • Strokes 5 Hz short gestures
