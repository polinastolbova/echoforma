01 / The idea

Not to illustrate a place.
Let it sound.

“What Does Armenia Sound Like?” is an artistic and technical exploration of generative sound for a museum environment. Rather than a finished musical composition, it proposes a continuous system in which timbre, density, and movement evolve over time. This is an initial concept, not a description of an installed museum system.

02 · SuperCollider · Drone / Wind

Drone and Distant Wind

A low harmonic layer moves within a narrow frequency range. Pink noise passes through a slowly changing filter, while occasional resonant impulses suggest distant bell-like overtones.

freq = LFNoise1.kr(0.03).range(180, 260);
drone = Mix([Saw.ar(freq),
  SinOsc.ar(freq * 1.5),
  SinOsc.ar(freq * 2)]) * 0.05;
wind = LPF.ar(PinkNoise.ar(0.03),

One idea.
Different languages.

The goal is not to decide which environment is “best,” but to explore which aspects of the same idea each one reveals: compositional structure, continuous sonic evolution, interactivity, or modularity.
